# Nature asset library

`nature.glb` holds Turku3D's city-landscape vegetation and fence kit, every asset with its LOD chain, sharing six
materials and their KTX2 textures. `nature.json` lists the per-asset numbers. `clouds.png` is the tileable noise of
the sky's cloud layer (`src/render/clouds.ts`). Look at everything in the viewer: `npm run dev`, then
`/nature.html` (`?view=asset|lods|gallery|forest&asset=tree_birch&time=13:00&wind=1`).

Nothing here is copied from a game or store. The geometry is generated by our own code
(`scripts/build-nature-assets.mjs`, `scripts/nature/`) or was modelled in Blender for this project. The textures are
our compositions of the CC0 scans listed below.

Shared textures (KTX2, embedded): foliage card atlas 2048² ETC1S (16 cards: eight trees, four shrubs, grass, wide
grass, meadow, ground rosette), bark column atlas 4096×512 ETC1S with a 2048×256 UASTC normal map (8 barks),
impostor atlas 2048² ETC1S (8 species × 3 side views + 1 top view) with a 1024² UASTC normal map, and 512² wood and
granite albedo/normal tiles. Draws per tree: bark + leaves at LOD 0 and 1, one impostor draw at LOD 2.

## How it is made

- **Leaf, needle and grass cards** (`scripts/nature/textures.mjs`): leaves are cut out of the scans by their opacity
  maps and placed by code along drawn twigs: alternate or opposite leaves, a back layer, darker leaves deeper in the
  cluster, a per-species brightness/saturation calibration to plausible albedo. Rowan and rose get compound leaves
  built from single leaflets. Spruce and juniper sprays come from the thuja-like sprigs of LeafSet019, Scots pine
  tufts from PineNeedles001 re-tinted green, grass clumps from blade scans, the meadow card adds drawn flowers.
- **Barks**: each scan scaled into a 448 px column with 32 px wrap gutters; luminance and saturation calibrated per
  species. The birch bark is the pale Bark009 scan pushed to chalk white with drawn tileable lenticels and black
  patches.
- **Trees and shrubs** (`scripts/nature/trees.mjs`): our own procedural skeletons per species (decurrent
  broadleaves with scaffold branches, branchlets and twigs; excurrent spruce whorls with hanging branchlets; Scots
  pine with a high irregular crown; multi-stem shrubs), tube bark meshes and bent leaf cards with crown-radial
  normals, vertex AO and per-vertex wind weights (`_WIND`: sway, flutter, phase). LOD 1 keeps the whole canopy as flat
  cards and cuts the bark; LOD 2 is three crossed planes and a top plane with baked albedo + normal
  (`scripts/nature/raster.mjs`, a CPU rasterizer).
- **Hedge, grass, ground cover**: a 2 m hedge module with an opaque leafy core wrapped in cards; crossed-card grass
  and meadow clumps; a flat dandelion rosette.
- **Fence kit** (picket fence, board fence, metal railing, park fence, granite kerb wall; 2 m modules with one post
  each, LOD 0 + LOD 1): modelled in Blender 5.2 through the Blender MCP server (mcp-for-blender 2.2.6, MIT, add-on
  pinned at `ahujasid/mcp-for-blender@992233bb`) by a headless Claude Code session whose only tools were the
  `mcp__blender__*` tools. The build reads its export (`blender/fence-kit.glb`, SHA-256
  `ebaa2608f13da3e54e5403e386412cee7444a165e5c232afac0a33e9ece6c0a0`) and binds the wood, metal and granite materials.
  Paint colour is per instance.
- **Clouds**: `scripts/nature/clouds.mjs` writes tileable Perlin fbm, inverted Worley billows, detail fbm and
  stretched cirrus streaks into the four channels. Project-owned.

## Rebuild

Run in `game/`. Raw sources live outside git (about 320 MB); the KTX2 encodes are cached there by content hash.

```bash
node scripts/nature/fetch-sources.mjs                      # ~/turku3d/data/assets-src/nature/{acg,polyhaven}
# Fence kit: ~/turku3d/data/assets-src/nature/blender/fence-kit.glb from the Blender MCP session (see above)
node scripts/build-nature-assets.mjs                       # writes nature.glb, nature.json, clouds.png here
```

The build is deterministic (seeded); previews of the atlases go to `~/turku3d/data/assets-src/nature/build/previews`.
