Style: Turku, south-west Finland (60.45 N), Nordic old-town riverside. Muted, earthy, slightly desaturated palette (ochre/yellow render, Turku ochre-red, sage and grey-green wood, off-white trim, grey granite). Dark green or near-black wooden window frames; casement windows with several panes; deep reveals; granite plinths with small cellar vents on old buildings. Clean but lived-in wear: slight weathering, faint streaks under sills, darker plinth top. Recognisably Nordic, never Mediterranean.
