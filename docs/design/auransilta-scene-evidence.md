# Auransilta connected scene evidence

The prepared office scene extends east to `[125,187.5,375,312.5]` in world-local
XZ. City Hall `1032958849` was already a complete approved asset; all eighteen
previous building records remain byte-identical. Three further approved buildings
come from the frozen `t0_1_1` product. The recipe pins all four source tiles,
material UV scales, asset dependencies, tree anchors and external provenance.

## Measured and mapped geometry

Auransilta bridge `526219736` has a 45.308 m mapped axis and a 12.635 m mapped
outline width. Seventeen lidar profile stations give absolute deck elevations
5.895–6.024 m. The source survey reports 2,753 held-out returns, median residual
11.5 mm and p90 residual 34.0 mm. The existing bridge producer adds its declared
20 mm surface offset, separate 140 mm raised sidewalks, source railings and
structural details. Slab thickness and unmeasured ornament remain type-based
priors, not surveyed dimensions.

The southeast lower passage follows mapped way `1524867396`; its source record
retains `access=no`, which is an access tag rather than a physical closure survey.
The northwest passage joins aligned mapped lower-path endpoints
`776088930+834588621`. The existing source passage producer supplies their floors,
portal aprons, vaults and masonry caps. The barrel shape and default 2.4 m clear
width / 2.7 m centre clearance are qualified structural priors. Their lower
floor anchors come from independent lower-walk profiles, never bridge-top lidar.
The explicit upper masonry-cap support shares the solid enclosure's vertices.

The ground source adds 21,522 representative points from a bounded dense raw
survey (988,195 accepted returns before spatial reduction). This restores the
backless office bench automatically under the unchanged 80 mm contact-spread
limit. No reference imagery or raw point arrays are published in the repository.

## Preserved vegetation and coverage

The three detailed vegetation assets retain 1,135 unique original source IDs,
rotations, species and crown metadata. Their bases are attached to final supports
using the original reference height. Another 1,272 unique trees remain in context,
for the same 2,407 total source identities. Fifty-two trunks in the playable area
have explicit solid colliders. Context geometry is recut outside the extended
bounds and excludes complete approved building footprints beyond that rectangle.

The final source audit checks 11,480 support samples against eight collision cells:
maximum contact discrepancy 0.076 mm, 89,168 collision triangles, and 0.00056 m²
uncovered source dry area. These are geometric consistency checks, not a visual
acceptance claim. Independent production-cell Rapier probes over both lower
passages and the upper deck found no missing support or steep/headroom failures;
visual comparison and a playable walking capture follow deployment.
