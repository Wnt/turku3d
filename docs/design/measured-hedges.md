# Measured hedges in prepared streets

A mapped hedge is a semantic line, not a tree bounding box. The source adapter
uses the existing OSM hedge and crossing-path geometry with raw class2/3/4/5
returns. Raw records and the frozen JSON stay in the local evidence cache;
`hedge-survey.json` is SHA-pinned by the scene recipe.

The original whole-line quantile admitted overhanging tree branches, leaving
only one of five pilot lines qualified. The new profile finds the lowest
continuous vegetation population above local ground. A0.5m height band must
have at least six returns in80% of2.5m stations. Adjacent qualifying bands are
one population; its strongest band supplies the existing crown-width/top fit.
A relative-height profile handles slopes when a level world-height profile
cannot qualify. Sparse bushes are rejected, rather than stretched into a hedge.

All five office/bridge pilot lines qualify:54 of56 stations support their
chosen crown populations. Their measured world tops are4.39,4.45,4.62,4.73 and
9.38m N2000; widths are0.62–0.75m. The last is on higher City Hall ground.
Source uncertainty remains0.15m. The larger frozen A3 window contains ten
qualified lines and one rejected line; only the current scene bounds compile.

Mapped path crossings remain openings. At most1m geometry segments attach all
four lower corners to the canonical support triangles. Level crown elevations
stay fixed; changing the ground must not lift the measured hedge top. Missing
support or a crown buried within0.15m of terrain rejects that segment.

The first prepared product contains121 solid segments over117.26m. Four City
Hall boundary/terrain pieces are rejected. The visible volume and collision use
the same vertices; the first real .col capsule crossing blocks with its correct
side normal and0.32m capsule-centre clearance. A parallel2.5m promenade route
stays grounded and unobstructed. All11,510 existing support samples retain the
0.076mm maximum collision discrepancy.

The retained64x64 procedural material preserves the old pigment. This slice
restores measured placement, height, ground contact and collision; it does not
claim individual-leaf realism. Organic edge/detail geometry is a separate
asset improvement. No building-specific image analysis is needed for this rule.
